# Select Coffee

Specialty coffee shop on 24th Street in the Mission District, San Francisco.

## Facts for anything we publish

- Beans: single origin, from three family farms in Oaxaca's Sierra Sur near Pluma Hidalgo. Shade grown at 1,400 m, hand picked. Roasted a few blocks away in small batches, 12 kg per roast, never more. Harvest runs November to February.
- At the bar: Cafe de olla $5.50 (brewed in clay with piloncillo and canela, the one we are known for), Oaxaca pour over $6.50, Horchata latte $6.00, Carajillo tonic $7.00 (cold brew, tonic, orange peel, mezcal free), Espresso $4.50, Pan dulce $4.00 (from our neighbors on 24th, daily). Oat, almond and whole milk at no extra cost.
- To take home: Sierra Sur beans, 12 oz, $22. Pluma Hidalgo natural, 12 oz, $22.
- Hours: Monday to Friday 7am to 5pm, Saturday and Sunday 8am to 4pm.
- Order ahead from your phone and pick it up at the window on 24th. No app, no account.
